A Physics-Driven Framework for Parametric Periodic-Flow Modeling and Finite-Amplitude Aeroelastic Response Analysis

arXiv 2026 33.5 method

TLDR

A physics-driven P-PINN framework models parametric periodic flows and finite-amplitude aeroelastic responses, validated on cylinder and airfoil forced-motion cases against time-marching simulations.

Reasoning

The paper's main strength is a purely physics-driven P-PINN that avoids pre-generated training data and transient simulations for parametric periodic flows, coupled with harmonic balance for aeroelastic response. It validates on numerical cylinder/airfoil forced-motion benchmarks, but the abstract lacks quantitative error metrics, generalization limits, and physical experiments. It is not about optimization, design, turbulence modeling, or LLM/agent workflows.
