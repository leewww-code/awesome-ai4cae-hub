Reliable and efficient steady CFD from surrogate predictions through Newton-Krylov correction

arXiv 2026 48.2 method

TLDR

A solver-coupled surrogate-Newton framework uses neural surrogate predictions as initial guesses for Newton-Krylov CFD, improving reliability and speed for transonic airfoil optimization.

Reasoning

The paper proposes a strong hybrid approach that couples neural surrogates with Newton-Krylov solvers to address out-of-distribution unreliability in steady CFD, with impressive residual reduction and speedup on airfoil optimization. It includes 2D transonic and 3D flying-wing empirical benchmarks, though details on datasets, baselines, and generalization are limited in the abstract. The work is relevant to CFD surrogate modeling and airfoil optimization but does not visibly involve LLM agents, turbulence modeling, or RANS-specific claims.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Solver-coupled surrogate-Newton framework that uses neural surrogate predictions as high-quality initial guesses for Newton-Krylov iterations to achieve accurate steady CFD
  • Combines rapid global flow-field prediction with high-accuracy numerical convergence at the terminal stage
  • Demonstrates surrogate-Newton correction on out-of-distribution geometries from actual transonic airfoil optimization trajectories
  • Extends the surrogate-Newton coupling to three-dimensional steady CFD using a flying-wing dataset

Methodology

The paper proposes a solver-coupled surrogate-Newton framework in which neural surrogate flow-field predictions initialize Newton-Krylov iterations, coupling global surrogate prediction with terminal numerical convergence. It is evaluated on an out-of-distribution benchmark of geometries sampled from actual transonic airfoil optimization trajectories, a practical supercritical airfoil optimization workflow, and a three-dimensional flying-wing dataset. Reported metrics include median residual L2 ratio, field and aerodynamic errors, online prediction reliability, and generation-level speedup relative to CFD.

Key Results

On the out-of-distribution benchmark, the framework lowers the median residual L2 ratio by over seven orders of magnitude and substantially reduces field and aerodynamic errors. In practical supercritical airfoil optimization, it improves online prediction reliability while achieving a 15.5-fold generation-level speedup over CFD.
