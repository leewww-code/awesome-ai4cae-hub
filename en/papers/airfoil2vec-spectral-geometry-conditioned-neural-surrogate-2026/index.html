Airfoil2Vec: Spectral Geometry-Conditioned Neural Surrogate Models for Airfoil Aerodynamics and a Downforce-Generating CFD Dataset

arXiv 2026 51.9 method

TLDR

Introduces a 10k RANS airfoil dataset and Airfoil2Vec spectral geometry-conditioned neural surrogate for fast pressure/velocity prediction, with generalization tests and CFD speedups.

Reasoning

The paper contributes a substantial open RANS dataset for downforce airfoils and systematically compares geometry-conditioned neural surrogates, including a novel spectral encoder with generalization tests. Its main limitations are that evaluations appear simulation-based and focused on 2D NACA geometries, with no direct airfoil design/optimization application shown in the abstract.

Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Publicly available dataset of approximately 10,000 RANS simulations of steady, incompressible, 2D subsonic flow over downforce-generating NACA 4-digit airfoils for automotive/motorsport regimes.
  • Airfoil2Vec spectral geometry encoder combining joint contour spectrum with separate spectral representations of camber and thickness.
  • Geometry-conditioned neural surrogate models for continuous pressure and velocity field prediction around airfoils.
  • Comparative evaluation of neural fields versus neural ODEs, MLPs versus graph-based models, and several spectral geometry-conditioning methods.

Methodology

The authors use a dataset of about 10,000 RANS simulations of steady, incompressible, two-dimensional subsonic flow around downforce-generating NACA 4-digit airfoils. They train geometry-conditioned neural surrogates to predict continuous pressure and velocity fields, comparing neural fields with neural ODEs, MLPs with graph-based models, and several spectral geometry-conditioning approaches. Generalization is tested on angle-of-attack interpolation, interpolation and extrapolation to unseen NACA 4-digit geometries, and unseen non-NACA airfoils.

Key Results

The resulting surrogate accurately captures aerodynamic quantities and qualitative flow features while providing orders-of-magnitude speedups over conventional computational fluid dynamics.

Limitations

  • Scope is limited to steady, incompressible, two-dimensional subsonic flow.
  • The dataset focuses on downforce-generating NACA 4-digit airfoils and automotive/motorsport aerodynamic regimes.
