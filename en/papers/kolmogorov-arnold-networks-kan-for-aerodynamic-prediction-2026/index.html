Deep Analysis

Innovations

  • Application of Kolmogorov-Arnold networks to aerodynamic surrogate modelling for predicting surface pressure distributions over subsonic and transonic airfoils.
  • A nuanced comparison of KANs against deep multilayer perceptrons and graph neural networks in fluid dynamics surrogate modelling.
  • Characterization of KAN trade-offs, including lower complexity and faster training but training instabilities and high hyperparameter sensitivity.

Methodology

The study compares KANs, MLPs, and GNNs on the task of predicting surface pressure distributions over subsonic and transonic airfoils. Evaluation focuses on predicting the whole pressure coefficients and interpolation across Mach numbers and angles of attack. Model complexity, training time, and predictive performance are compared.

Key Results

KANs show good performance in predicting whole pressure coefficients and interpolating across Mach numbers and angles of attack, but are comparable and marginally inferior to suitably trained MLPs. The best performance is achieved by a GNN at the cost of lengthier training, while optimal KANs have much lower complexity and faster training.

Limitations

  • KAN performance is only comparable and marginally inferior to a suitably trained MLP.
  • KANs suffer from training instabilities.
  • KAN performance is highly dependent on proper hyperparameter optimisation.
  • The best GNN performance requires lengthier training.
